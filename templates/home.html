About Nōgyō

Nōgyō is an AI-powered smart farming robot engineered for precision agriculture. Driven by an ESP32 micro-controller with GPS tracking, it analyzes soil nutrients via RS485 sensors while using machine learning to detect plant diseases and trigger automated spraying for targeted crop care.

About Nōgyō AI

Nōgyō AI is an AI-powered plant disease recognition system designed to help farmers and researchers identify plant diseases quickly using image-based deep learning technology.

By combining computer vision, machine learning, and agricultural intelligence, Nōgyō AI provides fast disease identification and supports smarter crop management.
